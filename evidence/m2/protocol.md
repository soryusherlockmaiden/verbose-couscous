## Settled requirements

Use OFFLINE execution, one shared simulated USDT wallet, and a maximum of one position across all symbols. No pyramiding. Strategy produces signals and intents. M1 authorizes quantities and processes fills, protective exits, funding and accounting under m1-replay-v8 and m1-v3. Keep one ledger across symbols. Starting and reference capital are 50 USDT with zero opening excess and external inflows. Observe the target at cycle equity ≥100 and the fixed halt at cycle equity ≤30. Recovery feasibility preserves a 50 USDT retained stake after a 50 USDT recovery, plus the studied reserve. Lifetime limits are two cycles and 100 USDT committed. These lifecycle values are fixed by PRD §§5.2–5.3. [Accepted M1 contract](../../SPEC.md#131-m1-offline-resolutions).

## SC-03 promotion gates

**PROPOSED.** Pre-register the full comparison family and a single primary hypothesis on the executable 50 USDT account. Net expectancy is mean realized net PnL per completed position round trip after fees, funding and adverse fills, with the wallet reconciled. Also compare cumulative net result on matched calendar blocks, including inactive blocks, so selective trading cannot hide time in cash. Freeze the end-of-window flat-settlement or censoring rule before comparing. Report 50 USDT eligibility over all causally generated pre-sizing signal attempts, including unassessable attempts, and separately over assessable attempts. Count only assessable passes as executable and disclose missingness, reasons and binding constraints. One reference-account report measures signal behavior independently.

| Criterion | Proposed design and basis for a numeric decision |
|---|---|
| Minimum effective sample and duration | Choose the effective-sample floor from the precision/power needed to distinguish the smallest economically meaningful net edge, using clustered development variation. Set a calendar duration floor that spans multiple regimes. A fixed minimum trade count plus a year-long duration is a simpler alternative, with less control over precision. Both numeric floors are OPEN. |
| Expectancy and uncertainty | Require a positive lower one-sided joint calendar-block bootstrap bound on net expectancy and incremental performance against the registered comparator; recommend testing a 95% bound and reporting economic effect size. A minimum economic margin above zero or a wider bound is stricter. Confidence level, marginal effect, comparator gate and threshold are OPEN. |
| Drawdown and ruin | Compare maximum decline from the running peak of observable cycle equity and settled loss tails. Report the observed worst drawdown and an upper uncertainty bound on ruin, proposed as modeled liquidation or settled equity ≤0. Also report fixed halt and undershoot separately. An alternative ruin threshold is bankroll below a feasible minimum order, which depends on the symbol pool. Maximum acceptable drawdown and ruin probability, and numerical tail criteria, are OPEN. |
| Cost stress and survival | Pair baseline and stressed fees, spread/slippage, funding and stop-gap scenarios with common calendar blocks and seeds. Recompute sizing and fills when costs change. Recommend primary net edge plus survival under plausible adverse joint stresses, using forward evidence where available. A marginal one-factor grid is easier to attribute and less protective against co-movement. Exact values, joint cases and survival thresholds are OPEN. |
| 50 USDT feasibility | Require executable-attempt rate and assessable coverage gates, with interval estimates and exclusions by symbol, regime and constraint. An executable rate among assessable attempts alone is an alternative that can hide missing coverage. Both floors and their uncertainty requirements are OPEN. |
| Multiple comparisons | Freeze the family across any timeframe, parameter set, comparator and exact operational reserve considered for promotion. Recommend Holm adjustment on one-sided prespecified hypotheses, with common joint blocks and interval construction consistent with the family decision. A single development-selected primary hypothesis plus secondary descriptive reporting conserves power. Family and error budget are OPEN. |
| Provenance | Require documented causal reconstructions, affected coverage and conservative sensitivity bounds fixed before evaluation. Exclude critical UNAVAILABLE symbol-periods. Report verified-only, reconstruction-dependent and reference-account evidence separately. Recommend verified evidence for production-critical filter/tier constraints until historical bounds are defensible. A forward-only evaluation trades breadth for stronger attribution. |
| Execution-model deviations | Record paired realized versus modeled closing fee and slippage dollars/bps and adverse stop residuals (gap, latency, missed or unprotected exit). Propose by-execution and aggregate tolerances calibrated to independent development observations, with severe protection failures reported and assessed separately. A single aggregate loss tolerance is easier to operate but masks execution failure modes. Freeze exact tolerances and quote basis. A later post-mortem breach suspends approval as in PRD §5.2.1. |

**PROPOSED decision rule.** PASS requires every frozen quantitative gate and adequate assessability, surviving registered stresses, plus a decision robust to approved reconstruction bounds. FAIL requires adequate evidence and a conclusive breach of a frozen gate (or a deterministic contract failure). INCONCLUSIVE covers insufficient effective sample/duration, material missing or disputed inputs, or an uncertainty interval that spans a threshold without clear breach. Predefine the gate-specific clear-fail and inconclusive regions, including how confidence bounds map to both outcomes. A tooling or governance dependency that prevents evaluation is BLOCKED. Paper/live eligibility is independently gated.

### Recovery reserve

The zero USDT baseline is settled as a required comparison. **PROPOSED** positive scenario estimates genuinely unmodeled closing-cost error using development evidence only. Pair each modeled close with an independently documented execution or executable depth quote at its modeled time, matched for side, quantity, fee basis and timestamp. Estimate adverse residual per close as observed minus modeled closing cost. Separately identify post-observation price movement, and omit fees, slippage allowance, posted cash and known accrued costs already charged. Keep units in USDT for the fixed research position sizing. Use a one-sided upper bootstrap confidence bound of a preregistered high quantile of the nonnegative residual by joint calendar blocks, round up to the approved USDT increment, and show sensitivity to quantile, thin depth and regime. A documented upper bound from independent depth and protection evidence is an alternative. Development must support a defensible positive amount. Tick increment, quantile/confidence, minimum independent episodes and value are **OPEN**.

**PROPOSED** designate the evidence-backed positive reserve as primary after it can be pinned. Designating zero as primary instead addresses baseline strategy economics while the positive scenario still tests structural shortfall. Primary designation is **OPEN**. Exact candidate operational values are **OPEN** and none is proposed without development evidence. Register each exact value in the comparison family before holdout if the operator wants that value available for M3. A different unregistered value needs a fresh holdout. ## SC-04 strategy and capital observation semantics

**PROPOSED development semantics.** Let bar i's interval end be its close time. Evaluate when its completed contract bar is first available, after the declared publication lag. Require the associated universe and every input to be observable at decision time. The strict crossings below use Decimal calculations. A valid intent can execute only at the first modeled open at or after both this availability and submission. With no modeled computation delay, zero candle lag can reach the open at the close boundary and any positive lag up to one interval generally reaches the following open. An alternative is to require a strictly later open even at zero lag. Record missing, late, stale and disallowed inputs with explicit reason codes and source bar IDs.

- Donchian upper/lower is max high/min low of bars i−20 through i−1. Require close i strictly above/below respectively. Including bar i would mechanically suppress strict breakouts.
- EMA(200) uses closes through i, seeded with the mean of the first 200 closes after a valid warm-up start, then E_i = 2/201 × close_i + 199/201 × E_(i−1). The baseline uses a chronological recursive stream within the epoch. A rolling warm-up reset at each timestamp is an exploratory sensitivity with different initialization.
- For ATR and ADX, TR is max of high−low, |high−prior close| and |low−prior close|. Positive DM is high−prior high when positive and strictly larger than prior low−low; negative DM is prior low−low under the mirrored condition. Equal moves set both to zero. Wilder smoothing initializes with 14 observations averaged, then W_i = (13 × W_(i−1) + x_i)/14. ATR is smoothed TR. Directional indices are 100 × smoothed DM/ATR. DX is 100 × absolute difference of the directional indices / their sum, zero if ATR or the sum is zero. ADX is Wilder-smoothed DX, seeded with the first 14 valid DXs. Require ADX strictly >25. A flat or zero-ATR history cannot size a trade.
- Prior volume is contract base volume at i−1. Require strictly >1.5 times the mean of i−21 through i−2. The filter's numerator and denominator do not overlap. Quote volume instead is an exploratory measurement alternative. Liquidity ranking uses quote volume.
- Require at least 200 contiguous completed signal bars and the separate proposed 60-day contiguous daily and funding history. Seed ATR/TR using changes from the second contiguous bar. A hole or relisting clears indicator state until fully rewarmed. Raise or explore a longer EMA stabilization period as a registered warm-up sensitivity. Never carry bar data across epochs.
- Set initial distance from bar i to 2.5 × ATR(14). M1 places the stop from the actual fill and rounds toward entry. Trail only from completed bars while the position survives. Use the favorable contract high since entry for longs and low for shorts, beginning at the entry fill, with the same ATR multiple. Ratchet in the safer direction under SC-02, where the old stop governs the intervening bar with positive lag and the next open's gap check. Test an alternative using closes as favorable extremes. No continuous intrabar ATR stop update.

**PROPOSED universe.** Reuse the M1 PIT universe, liquidity and history checks. Once per UTC day at a fixed offset (M1 feasibility used 00:01 UTC), use contiguous daily bars ending by the prior midnight and available by that cutoff. Rank by trailing 30-day mean quote volume and take the research candidate top 30, descending, breaking ties by symbol and listing epoch ascending. Union BTCUSDT/ETHUSDT that meet the same eligibility rules. Signed momentum is most recent completed close / the close 14 daily bars earlier − 1. Positive momentum supports longs and negative supports shorts. Zero supports neither. For a wallet conflict, propose ranking directional momentum magnitude descending within the eligible set, then liquidity rank, symbol and epoch, with rejected attempts retained. Document an operator-selected rule for fallback to a later simultaneous candidate if the first is infeasible. A liquidity-first tie policy would put more weight on estimated execution quality. Daily ranks should use a pinned observation offset and survive between cutoffs with current epoch and feasibility checks. Faster updates or a tighter universe are research variants. Hold an otherwise eligible open position through rank removal or sign reversal until its existing exit policy acts. No new entries in the removed direction. An exit at the next modeled open is an alternative with added turnover. Exchange close-only and delisting boundaries are protection constraints distinct from daily ranks, and unresolved exposed boundaries fail closed under M1.

**PROPOSED trigger cadence.** Order eligible snapshots by trigger_available_at and use the shared ledger event time and sequence as a secondary key. Null availability is audit-only. At each availability instant, process snapshots observable at that M1 stage before authorizing further entries. Within snapshots becoming available together, discard event keys older than the latest key evaluated at a preceding stage. Among the remaining group, prefer the fixed halt if any qualify. Otherwise latch target if any qualify. Use the earliest event key for the qualifying threshold within that group and record every decision, discarded late snapshot and multi-snapshot conflict. Snapshots emitted by a fill at that instant cannot determine an earlier authorization. An alternative is newest observable snapshot only, or bar-close-only checks, each with different missed-trigger bias. Compare with bar-close-only and, if approved evidence permits, a finer mark/event observation cadence. Match or exceed prospective live cadence before promotion. A coarser observation grid can hide drawdown or exaggerate overshoot. Keep later execution and settlement effects separate from the latched first decision.

**PROPOSED SC-02 sensitivities.** Pair positive- and zero-lag views of the same development data. First hold entry decisions and M1 policy fixed to isolate the trailing delay, then rerun all decision lags to measure the joint effect. Report exposure hours under old stops, gaps, exit price and time deltas, risk tails and target/halt outcomes. Separately aggregate M1 ambiguous settlements, adjustments, withheld/released credits and cash impact. Reprice observed holder candidates under the already documented exact-clock zero-window diagnostic where that pricing has the required settlement mark. Compare cash deltas on fixed exposure paths and label any feedback replay separately. The diagnostic is counterfactual and cannot replace the accepted adverse-candidate baseline. Missing marks limit the comparison.

## SC-05 minimal simulated Ignition

**SETTLED.** Evaluate only eligible M1 snapshots on conservative cycle equity. A gross mark ≥100 with cycle equity <100 does not trigger. A 50→80→45 path does not meet the fixed 30 halt. Block new entries on either first latch. Before entries, apply SPEC §8.1. Compute phase risk from cycle equity, then cap it by cycle equity minus halt, halt buffer and open risk. Open risk includes resting intents that can create exposure and excludes costs already deducted. Preserve effective budget and HALT_HEADROOM if no compliant quantity fits it. M1 rounds and fills through its authorization path. The T-30 0.50 buffer is a test input and does not approve a research buffer.

**PROPOSED.** On a latch, cancel pending entries and request an M1 reduce-only close at the first permitted modeled open. Leave the protective exit in force until flat. Use reconciled settled equity, with funding and fees posted once, against 100 + reserve inclusive. Track liquidation and gap undershoot distinctly. Treat an unobservable close or unreconciled result as censored by data, preserve reason codes and give bounds. Do not allow within-cycle top-ups or an automatic resume after shortfall. One alternative is to count every unresolved settlement as unsuccessful in a conservative bound. Simulated settlement addresses hypothetical recovery feasibility. Actual transferable balance is a later contract.

| Estimand | Proposed denominator and interpretation |
|---|---|
| Target before halt | Cohort of eligible fresh 50 USDT cycles started on preregistered calendar paths. First eligible target competes with fixed halt and terminal liquidation if it precedes both. Report count/fraction, competing risks by horizon and uncertainty. Open cycles at horizon are right-censored. Data failures are identified with separate adverse/optimistic bounds and excluded coverage tabulated. A start with valid data and no signal is still a cycle. |
| Conditional recovery feasibility | Among first-target cycles with observed settled equity, fraction ≥100 + reserve. All triggered cycles including unresolved ones enter lower/upper bounds. |
| Shortfall and dead-zone | Same settled-trigger denominator. Shortfall is settled equity <100 + reserve. Dead-zone is 100 ≤ settled equity <100 + reserve. Report per cost and reserve scenario and uncertainty, plus unresolved-trigger counts and bounds. The dead-zone interval is empty at reserve zero. |
| Lifetime recovery | Fraction of initiated experiments with at least one feasible settled recovery over ≤2 completed or censored cycles, cumulative capital ≤100, and a fixed calendar horizon. A hypothetical authorized restart may follow a reconciled fixed halt or separately modeled operator closure only. Apply residual to exactly 50 USDT and book the shortfall only as additional capital. A negative/unreconciled wallet requires an explicit model treatment and is not reset by assumption. Stop at feasible recovery. Base estimate uses consecutive joint calendar blocks and preserves regime dependence between cycles. Report no-restart and independent-cycle sensitivities; do not assume 1−(1−p)^2. |

Cycle start sampling, horizon, post-mortem restart model and conditional versus worst-case missing outcome reporting require registration. Avoid counting overlapping starts or correlated trades as independent. Report state/censoring counts per scenario and liquidations that happen before any observable halt. The future implementation uses these definitions for research outcomes exclusively.
