## Candidate pool and scoped acquisition

**PROPOSED.** Start coverage trials with BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT, the PRD's Major examples, and the later-delisted DGBUSDT shown by M1. DGB is a coverage probe with externally dated delisting evidence. Recruit the research pool within the approved pre-holdout scope from available USD-M USDT perpetual directories, date-limited exchange notices, and supplied captures. Record the discovery query and cutoff, when evidence became available, symbols tried and excluded, and gaps. Treat archive-derived membership and first-observed anchors as reconstructed. The same PIT requirements apply to majors and other symbols. The archives may omit expired, thin, renamed and short-lived contracts. Three observed symbols cannot establish survivor-free coverage or a tradeable dynamic universe. Alternatively, use only forward captures with full snapshots and dated listing notices, at the cost of sample size and regime diversity. [Protocol O1–O3](protocol.md#operator-decisions).

Approve the holdout boundary and chronological scopes before choosing assets. Use separate manifests and raw trees under ignored data/m2/ for development, validation and holdout. Keep holdout outside mounted development and validation roots. Pin canonical source URL, epoch, effective interval, compressed size, SHA-256, ingestion time and modeled delay. Obtain the exchange's published SHA-256 and size only for scope-approved archive URLs. Record effective/as-of evidence for supplied metadata. The M2 fetcher accepts canonical Binance Vision USD-M ZIP URLs. It derives the full UTC day or month from the URL before any request. The 48,000,000-byte limit applies both to the received archive and its single CSV member. Record total archive bytes, local sidecar bytes, exclusions and manifest digest.

If a boundary falls inside a month, check daily availability separately for contract klines, markPriceKlines, and fundingRate for every requisite symbol and interval on the approved side. Availability can differ by family and date. Daily contract availability alone does not establish daily funding coverage. No daily availability claim was made here. If any series is monthly-only at the split, leave the overlapping month sealed. Prefer aligning the boundary or shifting the usable decision dates. The operator may instead supply an attested development-only asset after its format and acquisition policy are approved.

The committed `history-seal-v1` record reserves [2025-01-01, 2026-01-01) UTC independently of M2 approvals. It applies to M1 and M2 history inputs by full source bundle, bounded settlement query, or capture receipt time. Inline metadata is sealed by observation time. Evidence observed at or after the start of a registered range also cannot describe an event time or span inside it, so a later backfill cannot report a sealed-period status, schedule or mark gap. A forecast or notice published before the range began stays admissible on both the inline and M0 capture paths, for example a `premiumIndex` next funding time at the first sealed instant. An instrument's onboard instant is an identity anchor and stays admissible. Anchor inline status evidence for an epoch onboarded inside a sealed range at or after the range end. Post-seal exchange snapshots still reveal which epochs survived a sealed range, so a sealed reserve is not a clean retrospective holdout. M1 skips sealed capture receipts before selecting payload BLOBs. Exact 2026 boundary receipts remain eligible in M1. M2 preflights the request and its contract, mark, daily-rank, warm-up and funding dependencies before payload access. Phase A reserves the full modeled horizon opening bar. Funding within the M1 ±15 s ownership window follows SC-02. Outside the window, coverage flags missing recorded marks by epoch, settlement and affected interval. The affected bar is excluded from reliable execution evidence, and M1 fails the replay if a position is exposed there. [Registered seal](../../src/acgs/history_seal.json)

For draft use, the trusted acquisition configuration must explicitly identify the designated holdout, approved decision and data ranges, canonical manifest digest, and history and funding dependency bounds. The registry is the sealing authority, and each range has a purpose: `m2-retrospective-reserve` or `m2-prospective-holdout`. An approved data window may lie after a reserve, such as 2026 forward evidence after the 2025 reserve, but never overlaps a registered range and ends before every registered holdout starts. The configured holdout can only tighten this: it may not truncate a registered range or start after a registered holdout. Each purpose still has a single approval and data window. The shipped draft has no market approvals. M2 refuses metadata with an effective time at or after the approved data-window end, even when the announcement arrived before the seal. Inline historic evidence and local settlement sidecars are digest-pinned and preflighted. For supplied captures use dated inline exports scoped to the approved window. Use trusted data directories. M2 resolves asset paths and rejects aliases. Windows 8.3 short-path behavior remains unverified.
